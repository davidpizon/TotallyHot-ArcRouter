# Arc Router vs. its source paper — deviation audit (read-only)
Repo: github.com/davidpizon/TotallyHot-ArcRouter (shallow clone of main, 2026-10-13). Paper: Zhou et al., "Agent-as-a-Router: Agentic Model Routing for Coding Tasks", arXiv:2606.22902 (read via arxiv.org/abs HTML).

## 1. Primary paper
- README.md:141-143 "evaluates against CodeRouterBench ... published by Zhou et al. alongside arXiv:2606.22902"; docs/score-delta-methodology.md:14 "Canonical reward in Zhou et al., arXiv:2606.22902 §3.2"; docs/research/paper-notes.md:1.
- Secondary: G-Eval (Liu et al., arXiv:2303.16634) README.md:322 for the judge; RouteLLM cited in ADR-0022; many judge papers in docs/research/code-quality-metrics-assessment.md.
- Note: docs/research/paper-notes.md calls the paper's system "TotallyHotArcRouter"; the paper calls it **ACRouter**. Notes are mislabelled (paper text: "We instantiate this framework as ACRouter").

## 3. Background claims checked
- Five abstaining voters: confirmed (VoterNames.cs, RoutingOptions.cs).
- Live vote ignores cost: confirmed for Orchestrator/Agent paths; UtilityRoutingPolicy is the exception.
- ~5% exploration with logged propensity: confirmed.
- Parser + G-Eval grading: confirmed; no execution.
- Leave-one-out frozen-baseline score delta: **incorrect** — score-delta exists, but without LOO.
- Local SQLite: confirmed (SqliteTranscriptStore, SqliteMemoryEntryStore, coderouterbench.db).
